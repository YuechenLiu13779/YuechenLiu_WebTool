Solid Wood Horn Chair Making

1. Background

What is it?

A solid wood horn chair.

Why make it?

  • Personal interest
  • To learn woodworking and the making process, rather than designing an original chair
  • To try making a complete chair

Design Source

The plan was found online and is based on an Italian-style chair design.

❗️HOWEVER❗️

  • The specific design period is unkown.
  • The original designer is unkown.

2. Materials

Main Materials

  • White Oak: chair legs, backrest, and main structure
  • Plywood, Leather, and Foam: seat

3. Basic Making Process

Choose and Prepare the Wood

First, use the plan to decide the size of the wood needed for each part:

  • Length of the chair legs
  • Size of the wood for the backrest
  • Size of the support pieces

❗️IMPORTANT❗️

The wood should be a little longer and thicker than the final size. This leaves extra material for cutting, sanding, and shaping class.

Cut the Wood

Cut the wood into the rough length needed for each part.

4. Making the Backrest

Step 1 – Cut the Wood

Prepare two large pieces of white oak.

Step 2 – Prepare the Surfaces

Use a planer to flatten one side of each piece of wood. This helps the two surfaces fit closely together.

Step 3 – Glue the Wood

Use wood glue to join the two pieces together. Leave them clamped for about 12 hours.

Step 4 – Draw the Backrest Shape

Use the plan to draw the size and curved shape of the backrest on the glued wood.

Step 5 – Cut the Rough Shape

Use a band saw to cut along the outline.

Step 6 – Refine the Backrest

Use a file and belt sander to remove extra wood, sharp edges, and uneven areas.

5. Making the Chair Legs

The chair legs need to be round

  • Draw the shape on the wood with chalk.
  • Put the wood on a lathe.
  • Slowly shape the wood into round chair legs.

❗️IMPORTANT❗️

The back legs need to be shorter than the front legs. This allows the chair to lean back when someone sits on it.

6. Joining the Chair Parts

There are two main ways to connect the chair parts.

1. Mortise and Tenon Joints

Step 1 – Make the Joints

First, make square holes in the chair legs. Leave some extra wood at the ends of the support pieces to form the tenons, then insert the tenons into the square holes, or mortises, in the chair legs.

Step 2 – Test the Connection

Put the parts together first to check if they fit correctly.

If the joint is:

  • Too tight
  • Not aligned
  • At the wrong angle

Use a file to adjust it.

2. Nail Connection

Nails are mainly used to connect the seat to the seat frame. Unlike mortise and tenon joints, no holes need to be prepared first, and the parts can be fixed directly with nails.

7. Making the Seat

Step 1 – Cut the Plywood

Use a table saw to cut the plywood into 4–5 strips.

Step 2 – Make the Curved Shape

  • Arrange the strips at the needed angles.
  • Glue them together with wood glue to create a curved seat.

Step 3 – Add and Shape the Foam

  • Place a square piece of foam on the seat.
  • Trim the foam to match the shape and thickness of the seat.

Step 4 – Cover the Seat with Leather

  • Pull the leather around the seat and toward the bottom.
  • Use a staple gun to fix one side first.
  • Then pull the other three sides tight and staple them in place.

❗️IMPORTANT❗️

The leather needs to stay tight and smooth, not loose.

Step 5 – Finish the Four Corners

  • After wrapping the seat, there will be extra leather at the four corners.
  • Use a knife to cut the extra leather into small triangle shapes.
  • This helps reduce folds and wrinkles around the curved edges.

Step 6 – Fix the Upholstered Seat

Finally, use a staple gun to secure the leather to the chair frame.

The finished edges should look:

  • Smooth
  • Tight
  • Rounded

FINISHED!!!

The solid wood horn chair is complete!
